Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

Bloom Block Offline is designed as a fully offline puzzle game. We respect your privacy and do not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal information.

Information We Collect

Bloom Block Offline does not collect personal information. The app does not require an account, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, microphone access, camera access, or any other personal data.

Offline Gameplay

The game works without an internet connection. It does not connect to our servers because no server is required for gameplay.

Local Game Data

The app may store gameplay progress locally on your device, including unlocked levels, best scores, star ratings, and audio settings. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Bloom Block Offline does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, or social login SDKs. The app does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Children's Privacy

Bloom Block Offline is a general audience puzzle game. Since we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties because we do not collect personal information.

Data Deletion

You can reset local game progress inside the app where available. You can also delete the app from your device to remove locally stored app data according to iOS behavior.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. Any future change that affects privacy will be reflected on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the support contact listed on the App Store product page.
